# 🐉 My Favorite Series: *Bakugan Battle Brawlers*

> "Bakugan, brawl! Bakugan, stand! Pyrus Dragonoid, stand!"
> — **Dan Kuso**

**Bakugan Battle Brawlers** is a Japanese-Canadian anime series that first aired in **2007**. It follows *Dan Kuso* and his friends as they battle using **Bakugan**, living creatures that roll up into small marble-like spheres and pop open into powerful monsters when thrown onto a Gate Card.

- The toys were *actually* playable in real life.
- The theme songs are ~~kind of cheesy~~ absolute bangers.

## 🌈 The Six Attributes

There are six attributes, and each has its own power:

- **Pyrus** – fire
- **Aquos** – water
- **Subterra** – earth
- **Haos** – light
- **Darkus** – darkness
- **Ventus** – wind
  - *Fun fact:* Ventus Bakugan are known for their speed.
  - Shun is the best Ventus brawler. (Fight me.)

## ⚔️ How a Brawl Works

Here is the basic flow of a brawl, written like a tiny program:

```python
def brawl(player, bakugan, gate_card):
    gate_card.set()
    bakugan.roll()
    if bakugan.lands_on(gate_card):
        bakugan.stand()
        return player.use_ability()
    return "Missed the Gate Card!"
```

In the show, every brawler shouts `"Gate Card, set!"` before the battle begins.
